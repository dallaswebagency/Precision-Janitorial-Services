    Do you provide cleaning supplies and equipment?

    Yes. We bring the supplies and equipment needed for the agreed scope, and any approved products you prefer can be built into your plan.

    Can cleaning be done after business hours?

    Yes. Schedules are set around your access and operating hours, including evenings and early mornings.

    Do you offer one-time and recurring cleaning?

    Yes. We handle recurring programs, periodic deep cleans, move-in/move-out cleaning and post-construction work.

    How is quality controlled?

    Through a defined scope, a facility checklist, supervisor oversight, issue follow-up and regular scope reviews.

    Which areas do you serve?

    Alberta, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, and Newfoundland and Labrador. Final availability is confirmed during the quote process.
